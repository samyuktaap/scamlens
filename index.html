FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Rules decide. AI explains." mean? +
Verdicts (Safe, Suspicious, Dangerous) and risk scores (0–100) are determined 100% deterministically by local heuristic rules and verified lookups. Gemini is never permitted to invent risk scores or decide verdicts—it is used exclusively to translate cryptic cybersecurity evidence into plain, friendly language for everyday users.
Does ScamLens work completely offline? +
Yes! All core signals (Levenshtein brand typos, Punycode IDN homographs, raw IP hosts, suspicious TLDs, and cross-domain forms) evaluate locally in under 5 milliseconds with zero network required. Offline template guidance activates automatically if internet access is unavailable.
Does ScamLens track or send my browsing data? +
No. ScamLens is built with privacy-by-design. Only target hostnames are queried during optional deep checks, and full URLs are never stored or logged on remote servers. Zero personal credentials or cookies ever leave your device.
Are any paid APIs or credit cards required? +
None! ScamLens runs exclusively on free developer tiers: Google AI Studio (Gemini Flash), Google Apps Script Web App, and Google Public DNS over HTTPS.

📦 Install ScamLens in Chrome

Follow these 3 quick steps to load ScamLens as an unpacked Chrome extension:

Step 1: Download & Extract

Download scamlens-extension.zip. Right-click it and choose "Extract All" to your Desktop or Downloads folder.

Step 2: Open Extensions in Chrome

Open Chrome and navigate to chrome://extensions. Toggle the Developer mode switch in the upper-right corner.

Step 3: Load Unpacked Folder

Click "Load unpacked" in the upper-left corner. Select the extracted extension directory (containing manifest.json).
